## Backward Incompatibilities {#sec-nixpkgs-release-27.05-incompatibilities}

- `mopidy` has been updated from 3.x to 4.x.
  Third-party extensions must support Mopidy 4.
  See the [upstream changelog](https://docs.mopidy.com/stable/changelog/) before upgrading.
  `mopidyPackages.mopidy-tidal` and `mopidyPackages.mopidy-ytmusic` are marked broken because they are not yet compatible.

<!-- To avoid merge conflicts, consider adding your item at an arbitrary place in the list instead. -->

- `mangohud` now names its Vulkan layers as upstream does, `VK_LAYER_MANGOHUD_overlay_x86_64` and `VK_LAYER_MANGOHUD_overlay_x86`, instead of `VK_LAYER_MANGOHUD_overlay_64_x86_64` and `VK_LAYER_MANGOHUD_overlay_32_x86`.
  Configurations that name these layers in `VK_INSTANCE_LAYERS`, `VK_LOADER_LAYERS_ENABLE`, `VK_LOADER_LAYERS_DISABLE` or a Vulkan loader settings file need the new names.
